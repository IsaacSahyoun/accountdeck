AccountDeck 2 for Mac

Use your own Claude and Codex accounts on your own Mac login.
Requires Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS Sonoma 14 or newer, and internet access.
Install Claude Code, Claude Desktop, or Codex first. You can use any of them on its own.

1. Double-click the zip in Finder to unzip it.
2. Open AccountDeck2-mac and double-click Install AccountDeck.command.
3. Wait for “AccountDeck 2 is installed.” The dashboard and menu bar icon appear.
   Allow the named login Keychain items when prompted to save your logins.

If macOS blocks the shortcut:
This download is unsigned and unnotarized. For a copy you trust, right-click the
shortcut, choose Open, then Open again. On newer macOS, try opening it first,
then use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

Still blocked? Open Terminal, type cd followed by a space, drag this unpacked
folder into Terminal, then press Return. Run this one line:

    bash install.sh

Or remove this trusted package's quarantine flag, then open the shortcut again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine .

Do not use sudo. Do not disable Gatekeeper system-wide.

First run:
- Claude Code: your current login is saved once verified. Use Add Account for more.
  Finish the sign-in in its new Terminal window, then close that window.
- Claude Desktop / Codex: choose Save Current Account, or Add Account.
  Codex needs file-based login storage. README.md explains how to set it.
- Choose Switch beside a saved account. Review the plan before continuing.
- Auto-switch applies only to Claude Code. It starts on at 95% used and needs
  two accounts. Change it or turn it off in Settings > Auto-Switch.
- Use AccountDeck to change accounts. Never use /login, /logout, or the client's
  Log Out to switch saved accounts: these can revoke a saved sign-in.

Uninstall:
Quit AccountDeck 2 from its menu bar menu. Double-click Uninstall AccountDeck.command.
It keeps your saved accounts and settings. See README.md to erase data too.

Your data stays in ~/Library/Application Support/AccountDeck2.
The vault is encrypted with a key in your login Keychain. Sign-in and usage
checks contact the providers; AccountDeck has no cloud account or telemetry.
Share the zip, never your data folder. See README.md for more help.
