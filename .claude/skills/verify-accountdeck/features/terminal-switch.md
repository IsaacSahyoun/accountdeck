# Claude Code accounts: switch, auto-switch and Undo

Status: driven 2026-11-12 through `drive.py` in the WebKit window (the journey below), on the app code of commit 5c9b5da. The tests named here are in the final check.

Client id `claude_cli`. Every open terminal follows the one account in use. A switch writes Claude Code's login file and its name file as one step, under a write-ahead record.

## Sub-features
- Switch to a saved account from its row. A sheet asks first.
- Auto-switch: when the account in use reaches the threshold, the app switches to the next one in the order shown in the Auto-switch card. An Undo is offered for 10 minutes.
- The early switch (Settings, "Use weekly allowance before it resets", on by default): while the account in use has room, the terminals move to the usable account whose week ends soonest, however far off that end is, so allowance is spent before it expires. Until 2026-11-14 it moved only to a week that ended within 24 hours. The target is an account a switch at the threshold could go to (a reading at most 15 minutes old, 10 points of room below the threshold in its weekly and its Fable limit) with at least 20% of its week unused. A week that has not started is not moved to, and neither is a week that ends within the hour of the week in use: such weeks end together, as they do in the order. Of several weeks that end within the hour of the soonest, the pick is the one with the most room. The default order breaks a tie of two weeks the same way. Its tie is pairwise, so with three weeks that each end within the hour of the next, and the first and the last further apart, which account the order puts first depends on the order the store lists them in, and it can differ from the early switch's pick. The 2.1.0 build does the same. To leave the account in use, the other week has to end sooner by the hour and by 5 minutes more (`SAME_RESET`): a reset time differs by a second from reading to reading, and without that margin two weeks an hour apart could trade places once. An account whose reading is too old is passed over, not waited for: waiting was tried and taken out, because an account that cannot be read would hold every early switch back. It waits 2 hours after any automatic switch (`last` in `cli_history`, whatever its kind) and after an early switch that was started and failed (`early_tried`, written by `cli_auto.tick`). So a switch the account in use needed is not followed by a second change of login a check later, and its Undo stays. The page says that wait ("The early switch waits 2 hours after an automatic switch") only while it holds a move back. It follows the weekly limit's reset time, not the Fable limit's. Where a switch at the threshold goes is the order's to say, with the early switch on or off. For a few commits on 2026-11-14 such a switch went straight to the early switch's target; that was taken out after a review, because it put a first choice ahead of the user's order that Settings did not show and it had its own gaps. Two hours after an Undo of an early switch the same move is made again.
- An automatic switch that does not happen is not started again at the next check (until 2026-11-14 it was, every 5 seconds, with a call to renew the login and an alert each time). `CLISwitch.run` notes the start of every automatic switch in the history before it does anything else (`cli_auto.note_try`, `cli_history["tried"]`: per account, the time and which try in a row it is) and takes the note out when the switch worked. A try counts on from the last one within 15 minutes of a first try and within the hour of a later one. A check also takes out the note of the account in use, with usage checks paused too. After a first try the rule waits 90 seconds (`cli_policy.AGAIN`) with the terminals where they are, and the same switch is tried once more: most failures are not the account's and are over by then. The wait belongs to that account: when a new reading puts another account first, the terminals move there at once. Every automatic switch asks the provider itself whose login is live (`CLISwitch.run` forgets a lookup that failed), so a second try does not fail on a lookup that failed under a minute before. While a switch runs, the page is given no hold. After a second try in a row the account is passed over for 15 minutes (`cli_policy.RETRY`) for every kind of switch, with the row words "switch tried 3 min ago", and the next account is used. An account with room is waited for while the account in use still works, and the same hold shows when nothing else is left, in place of "All terminal accounts are low": "A switch to Beta was tried and is tried again in 9 min at the earliest". A note counts only for an account the decision would use without it, so one whose Fable limit ran out does not hold that alert back while the account in use still works. "At the earliest", because a switch also needs a new reading, and after a refused renewal of its login the app does not read an account for 30 minutes. A switch by hand is not noted. A switch that is cancelled because Claude Code changed accounts under it counts as tried. A failed early switch moves to no other account: the early switch waits its own 2 hours.
- A choice the user made by hand is kept for 2 hours, unless that account's week runs out or it needs sign-in. Once auto-switch has moved the terminals off the chosen account, nothing is held.
- Leave an account out of auto-switch, and put it back (More menu, Leave Out of Auto-Switch).
- The login Claude Code holds is saved by itself the first time it is seen.
- A terminal switch can run beside another client's "switch when idle" wait.

## How to get to it (user's view)
- Dashboard, "Claude Code accounts": the Switch button on a row, then Switch Now in the sheet.
- The Auto-switch card on the left: the toggle, and the gear for the threshold.
- The notice after an automatic switch: Undo.
- Menu bar item: "Switch Claude Code To", then an account.

## Driving it
The journey, on the `terminal` scenario (Beta is in use there):
```sh
"$PY" -E -s tools/verify/drive.py "$PY" "$COPY" terminal "$PROOF" '[
  {"accounts": true},
  {"shot": "1-before"},
  {"click": "Switch", "row": "Alpha", "client": "claude_cli"},
  {"wait": "document.getElementById(\"modal\").open"},
  {"words": "sheet"},
  {"click": "Switch Now", "in": "sheet"},
  {"wait": "document.getElementById(\"modal\").innerText.includes(\"Switched to Alpha\")"},
  {"words": "sheet"},
  {"shot": "2-report"},
  {"click": "Done", "in": "sheet"},
  {"wait": "!document.getElementById(\"modal\").open"},
  {"row": "Alpha", "client": "claude_cli"},
  {"row": "Beta", "client": "claude_cli"},
  {"accounts": true},
  {"shot": "3-after"}
]'
```
It works when the sheet goes from "Working…" to the report "Switched to Alpha", the second `accounts` line shows `('claude_cli', 'Alpha', 'in use')` and Beta no longer in use, the Alpha row carries the "In use" badge, and the Beta row has a Switch button again.

## Gotchas
- In `terminal` the account in use is Beta, and in `typical` it is Alpha. The row of the account in use has no Switch button.
- The sheet does not close after Switch Now. It shows "Working…" with a Hide button, then a report, "Switched to <name>", with Explain Last Switch and Done. A journey waits for the report and clicks Done.
- An Undo planned right after an automatic switch is refused until that switch has ended ("Finish the current operation or recovery first"). A test waits for `runner.free_for("claude_cli", beside_waits=True)` first.
- `engine.sense_active()` must run before the policy is asked anything.
- A running demo acts on the early switch about 10 seconds after it starts, when the week of its account in use is not the one that ends first, and minutes later when its script makes it so (a refill in `resetting`, the end of the incident in `outage`). The scenarios `resets-soon`, `refilled`, `fable-binding`, `resetting`, `outage` and `fifty` turn the setting off so they hold still (`fixtures._HOLDS_STILL`), and `ScenarioTest.test_no_demo_moves_its_terminals_by_itself` moves every scenario's clock on for 25 minutes. The `typical` demo still leaves Alpha at the threshold about 10 minutes in, as it did before. To watch the early switch in a demo, drive `refilled` and turn the setting on in Settings.
- Auto-switch acts on an account's weekly limit and its Fable weekly limit, and on no other model's (a `pinned_model` setting adds that model's, and no control sets it). The 2.0.0 build acted on the weekly limit alone while Settings and the notch named both. A reading has a Fable limit only after the first Fable message, and an account without one is judged on its week. An account with less than 10 points of room in its week or in its Fable limit is refused as a target. When every account is like that, the one in use is used up to 99% and then left for an account with both limits at 97% or less. An account whose Fable limit is above 97% is never used while the account in use still works. When the account in use works for no model (its week ran out, or it needs sign-in) and no account can be used up that way, the rule moves to one whose week can still be used, picked in the user's order by its week, and it does not move back. That switch keeps the kind of its trigger (`out` or `login`), also after "All terminal accounts are low". A manual choice, Undo included, holds for 2 hours against a Fable limit that ran out, and not against a week that ran out. The hold is for the chosen account, which the history names (`manual_for`, written with `manual_at` by `CLISwitch`): once auto-switch has moved the terminals off it, nothing is held (`cli_policy._held`). A history from before `manual_for` holds whichever account is in use. The read schedule counts an account in use as used up only when its week is. An account past the threshold, or one used for its week alone, is used only on a reading at most 15 minutes old. An older one is read again ("Checking Beta before switching"), and an account with a new reading behind it in the order is used first. An account with room and an old reading is waited for while the account in use still works. Once the account in use works for no model, an account that is ready is used at once. `cli_auto.tick` switches on a reading at most 3 minutes old (`cli_policy.READY`) and reads an older one again first. Until 2026-11-14 a first choice whose reads Claude had paused kept the accounts behind it waiting until its reading was 15 minutes old, also when the account in use had run out. Now an account whose reading is over 3 minutes old, and whose reads are paused, is skipped like an account with an old reading ("Claude paused its checks" in the order the page shows), for every kind of switch, while the pause lasts. A pause lasts 15 to 60 minutes. With no other account ready the rule waits for the pause to end, and the hold says so ("Claude paused the usage checks of Beta") where it said "Checking Beta before switching": no check runs before then. The read is asked for all the same, and the scheduler starts it when the pause is over. That wait also holds the terminals on an account that has run out, as the 2.1.0 build does. The rule goes by the pause the store keeps (`paused_until`, which `cli_accounts._read` sets when Claude answers HTTP 429 and clears when a read works). While it lasts the scheduler starts no read of the account and the Refresh button is refused, so a wait for a new reading cannot end. A read that fails without a pause is tried again and waited for, as in the 2.1.0 build. Two of those cannot work for 30 minutes and are waited for all the same, until the reading is 15 minutes old: a Cloudflare block, and a login renewal that was refused (`_refresh` answers "checked recently" without asking Claude). The rule does not go by the row's `error`, which a save of the login clears while the reads stay paused, and every switch saves the login of the account the terminals leave. It does not go by the time of the last try (`flags["checked"]`) either: another writer of `flags` can put an older copy back, and that time and the reading's time come from two reads of the clock. `SoonestWeekFirstTest.test_an_account_whose_reads_are_paused_is_passed_over` holds the rule and both hold sentences. `TerminalSoonestWeekTest` holds it through the engine with a read that Claude refuses: as it is, with a login save after the refusal, with an older copy of `flags` written back, with the clock set back during a read that works, and through the app's own scheduler, which reads the account again only once the pause is over. What the rule stands on has tests of its own: `test_nothing_reads_an_account_while_its_reads_are_paused` (the Refresh button and a refresh of every account) and `tests.test_readings.SchedulerTest.test_a_paused_account_is_not_read_until_its_pause_ends_whoever_asks`. Claude's answer can give the Fable limit under a named key (`seven_day_fable`) and in its list, where the model's name can carry a version ("Fable 5.1", saved as `seven_day_fable_5_1`). They are one limit, and the app names it "Fable" in both cases (`usage.model_label`): the label is "Fable weekly", and the window keeps Claude's own name for the model in `model`. Older builds named it "Fable 5.1", and a reading one of them saved is shown under the new name (`usage.saved_reading`), while a line in the Activity list keeps the words it was written with. The list's entry is the one that counts, and each keeps its own id, because the forecast reads the saved samples of an id as one series. A reading's `raw_windows` says which limit came from a named key (`usage._from_keys`), so two list entries of one model stay two limits. The rule then acts on both, and the page and the menu bar show the first one (`AD.accounts.fableWindow`, `pool._windows`). No answer that names a model twice has been seen. `tests/test_readings.py`, `FableLimitTest` and the decision table hold the rule, `tests/test_switch_safety.py`, `TerminalFableLimitTest` holds it through the engine, and section D of `policy_probe.py` shows it. Claude has no 5-hour limit. The 2.0.0 build needed a 5-hour number from every account: with none in Claude's answer it skips every other account as "reading incomplete", never switches, and the menu bar counts no account. `tests/test_readings.py`, `ClaudeHasNoFiveHourLimitTest` holds the fix, and section C of `policy_probe.py` shows it on Claude's own answers.
- Open, and in the installed 2.2.0 too: after Claude refuses to renew a login that runs out within about 2 minutes, the app does not read that account for 30 minutes. A switch needs a reading at most 3 minutes old, so a try that needs a new one never starts, the page shows no hold, and the terminals stay on an account that ran out until the reading is 15 minutes old and the next account is used. A second try 90 seconds after the first avoids this only when the reading was under 90 seconds old at the first try. The runs are `lead/opus-1-*.txt` in `~/.accountdeck/reviews/failed-switch-round-2-2026-11-14/`.
- The dashboard and the menu bar do not offer a manual terminal switch during another client's wait. The engine supports it. That is an open decision, not a bug.
